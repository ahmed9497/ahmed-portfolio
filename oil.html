Who We Are
The UAE's Pioneer
in Used Oil Recycling

Dure Oil Middle East Establishment is a state-of-the-art zero emission re-refinery located in the heart of Abu Dhabi Industrial City (ICAD I), proudly holding the distinction of being the sole modern used oil recycling facility in the United Arab Emirates.

We specialize in the re-refining of used engine oil, crude sludge, and heavy hydrocarbon-structured oil — transforming these materials into high-quality re-refined base oil and fuel oil through advanced distillation technology.

Our facility operates under the highest environmental standards, ensuring that every barrel processed results in zero harmful emissions — contributing directly to the UAE's Vision 2030 sustainability goals and the circular economy.
