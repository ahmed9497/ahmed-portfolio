FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to buy a home?

On average, the home buying process takes 3 to 6 months from start to close. This includes searching, making an offer, inspections, financing, and closing. I'll guide you through every step to keep things moving smoothly.

What are your commission fees?

My standard commission rate is competitive and in line with the current market. I'm always transparent about fees upfront so there are no surprises. Let's discuss your specific situation — contact me for details.

Do I need to get pre-approved for a mortgage?

Yes — getting pre-approved shows sellers you're a serious buyer and strengthens your offer. I work with trusted lenders and can help connect you with the right one based on your situation.

Can you help with property investment advice?

Absolutely. I analyze market trends, rental yields, and property appreciation to help you make smart investment decisions. Whether it's your first investment or your tenth, I've got you covered.
